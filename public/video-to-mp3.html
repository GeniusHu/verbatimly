Questions people actually ask.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no daily limit, no watermark. The conversion runs in your browser, so it costs us almost nothing to offer.

Do I have to upload my video?

No. Your browser extracts the audio track locally. The video never leaves your device, which is also why there is no upload progress bar to wait for.

Which formats work?

MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM and AVI. If a MOV or MKV fails to decode, export it as MP4 first and try again.

What bitrate do I get?

128 kbps MP3 at the video's original sample rate, with up to two channels.

How long can the video be?

30 minutes or 500 MB, whichever comes first. Encoding happens on your device, so longer files take noticeably longer.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes on modern iOS and Android browsers. Very long videos can exhaust memory on older phones — trim first if the tab reloads.
