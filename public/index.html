Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You get 3 files per day, each up to 30 minutes, with no account required. TXT, SRT and VTT export are all free. Speaker separation, files over 30 minutes and batch upload are on the paid plan.

Do I need to sign up?

Not for the free tier. No email, no account, no credit card.

Which video and audio formats can I upload?

Video: MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI. Audio: MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, AAC. Over 100 languages are detected automatically, so you don't have to pick one.

How long can a video be?

Free: up to 30 minutes and 500 MB per file. Paid: up to 10 hours and 5 GB per file.

How fast is it?

Transcription runs on our servers, so there is no AI model to download and nothing to install. A 10-minute video is usually done in under a minute.

Can it tell who is speaking?

Yes, with speaker separation (paid plan), each segment is labelled Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on, with timestamps.

Can I export subtitles?

Yes — TXT, SRT and VTT subtitle files are all free, ready to upload to YouTube or any video editor.

What happens to my files?

Uploaded files and transcripts are deleted automatically within one hour. They are never used to train models and never reviewed by a human.
