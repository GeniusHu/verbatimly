Questions people actually ask.

Is it really free?

Three files a day, up to 30 minutes each, no account. The transcript and every export are free. Speaker labels, longer files and batch upload are paid.

Do I need to sign up?

No email, no account, no card. Drop a file and take the transcript.

What happens to my file?

Deleted within one hour, never used to train models, never read by a person.

Which formats work?

MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM and AVI for video; MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG and AAC for audio. If a MOV fails to decode, export it as MP4 first.
